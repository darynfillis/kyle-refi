Recommended Loan Structure — Executive Summary
8-Property IO Portfolio Refinance  |  Daryn Fillis, NEO Home Loans  |  NMLS #1988371
All rates subject to market conditions & final underwriting
Kyle, The portfolio is carrying a blended rate of 7.655% across $10.87M of debt. The assets are performing and the structure is functional. The cost of capital is not. A straight rate and term refinance reduces the blended rate into the high 6% range and improves cash flow by $6,970 per month, or $83,600 annually. No change to leverage, no complexity. The proposed structure goes further by allocating loans across multiple lenders based on pricing and exposure limits. This achieves a lower cost of capital at the portfolio level than a single-lender execution. Total debt increases modestly due to financed closing costs, but the blended rate lands in the mid-6% range and cash flow improves by $3,951 per month. On top of that, $379,071 in net proceeds comes back at closing after costs are covered, capital you can put to work. The structure does three things: lowers the effective cost of capital across all eight loans, maintains interest-only flexibility with short prepayment terms, and distributes lender exposure to achieve better pricing at each position. On timing, staying put costs $6,970 per month relative to available execution today. A 60 to 90 day delay is $14,000 to $21,000 in additional interest expense with no structural benefit. A 25 basis point rate improvement would reduce monthly interest cost by approximately $2,200. At that savings rate, it takes 6 to 9 months just to recover the cost of waiting, assuming that improvement materializes and is reflected in DSCR pricing, which is not guaranteed. The outcome here is driven by structure, not by rate timing. The execution improves the cost of capital immediately and can be adjusted if market conditions shift. Ready when you are.
